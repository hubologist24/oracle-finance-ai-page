Enterprise Security & Privacy

This tool is designed with strict enterprise compliance boundaries in mind:

  • Strictly Read-Only: Connects using a dedicated, restricted user. It cannot modify your data or alter underlying ledger tables.
  • Local Data Execution: Your actual financial data never leaves your network. The AI engine only structures the SQL query; execution happens entirely inside your private network session.
  • Sandbox Safe: Ideal for testing on your UAT, QA, or Oracle VISION demo environments.
