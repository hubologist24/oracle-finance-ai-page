Oracle Finance AI

AI-powered Oracle Finance query assistant that converts natural language business questions into executable Oracle SQL queries.

Example Questions

  • How much have we spent so far this year?
  • Which vendors are costing us the most money last year?
  • How is our spend distributed across different currencies for 2025?
  • Is our monthly spend growing or shrinking from 2024 to 2025?
  • Top 5 vendors working with at least 2 currencies
  • Best 2 vendors for each currency
  • Highest rejected PO months

How It Works

The system uses AI to:

  • Understand business questions
  • Generate Oracle SQL dynamically
  • Execute queries securely
  • Return formatted analytics and results
